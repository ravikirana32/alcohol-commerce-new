# P76–P100 roadmap

## P76 — Multi-state excise configuration

Jurisdiction profiles, state-specific product classifications, permitted outlet activities and effective-dated rule packs. Keep Karnataka as the initial profile; do not infer legal permissions.

## P77 — Regulatory reporting automation

Configurable report schedules, source-data lineage, validation, export snapshots and submission evidence tracking. Submission remains a human-authorised action.

## P78 — Licence lifecycle management

Licence records, document versions, expiry reminders, renewal workflow, outlet/activity mapping and suspension controls.

## P79 — Compliance rules engine

Versioned rules, effective dates, jurisdiction scope, explainable decisions, test cases and fail-closed behaviour for unknown rules.

## P80 — Compliance audit centre

Evidence register, immutable audit events, exception queues, reviewer decisions and exportable audit packs.

## P81 — Enterprise hierarchy management

Group, region, legal entity, outlet and operational-unit hierarchy with scoped access and consolidated views.

## P82 — Central procurement platform

Purchase planning, requisitions, approval matrix, supplier comparison and centrally negotiated catalogues.

## P83 — Advanced warehouse management

Warehouse locations, bin-level stock, receiving, put-away, picking, cycle counts and dispatch controls.

## P84 — Supply chain visibility

Purchase-to-delivery milestones, shipment events, transfer visibility, exception alerts and traceability.

## P85 — Enterprise business intelligence

Governed semantic metrics, multi-entity dashboards, scheduled extracts and row-level access controls.

## P86 — AI demand forecasting

Opt-in forecasting with explainable assumptions, historical data quality checks and human approval; AI remains disabled by default.

## P87 — Intelligent inventory optimisation

Reorder recommendations, safety-stock scenarios and expiry/slow-moving insights with operator approval.

## P88 — Fraud and anomaly intelligence

Rule-based anomaly signals, case management, evidence links and false-positive review; no automatic punitive action.

## P89 — AI business assistant

Permission-aware question answering over approved business data, citations to source records and explicit AI opt-in.

## P90 — Automated business recommendations

Prioritised recommendations with rationale, expected impact, confidence and human acceptance/rejection tracking.

## P91 — Enterprise mobile applications

Role-based mobile workflows for managers, field teams and outlet operators with secure device lifecycle.

## P92 — Supplier and partner integration hub

Partner API catalogue, credentials vault integration points, webhooks, retries, idempotency and health monitoring.

## P93 — Advanced brand analytics

Campaign reach, outlet participation, sell-through proxies, incentive cost and privacy-aware aggregated insights.

## P94 — Enterprise API marketplace

Versioned API catalogue, developer onboarding, scopes, quotas, keys lifecycle and usage analytics.

## P95 — Customer engagement platform

Consent-centred communications, preference management, suppression lists and jurisdiction-specific channel controls.

## P96 — International localisation

Locale, currency, timezone, tax and measurement configuration with jurisdiction-specific product and compliance packs.

## P97 — Global enterprise deployment

Regional deployment topology, data residency boundaries, regional operations and disaster-recovery design.

## P98 — Advanced platform security

Centralised security posture, key rotation workflows, privileged access reviews, threat detection and incident evidence.

## P99 — Platform ecosystem marketplace

Governed extension directory, partner certification workflow, permission review and tenant-controlled installation.

## P100 — Global platform maturity

Consolidated operational scorecards, SLO governance, release governance, resilience exercises and enterprise maturity assessment.
