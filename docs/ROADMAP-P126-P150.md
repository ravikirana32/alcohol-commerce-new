Audit and implement outstanding P00–P125 functionality with traceable requirements and acceptance evidence.

Verify retail, bar, inventory, procurement, finance and ecosystem workflows across roles and outlet modes.

Implement versioned migrations, preflight checks, tenant-safe upgrades, rollback strategy and migration evidence.

Complete API contracts, frontend integration, validation, accessibility, resilient states and observability.

## P130 — Technical debt elimination

## P139 — Vendor and partner ecosystem

## P140 — Advanced business intelligence

## P141 — Multi-region cloud architecture

## P142 — High availability and disaster recovery

## P143 — Global security operations

## P144 — Advanced integration ecosystem

## P145 — Global compliance management

## P146 — Enterprise white-label expansion

## P148 — AI operations intelligence

## P149 — Global customer expansion

Maturity assessment, governance, SLAs, product lifecycle and long-term platform strategy.

## Acceptance rules

- Complete UI/API/database workflows, not only schemas or flags.
- Trace requirements to automated tests and operational evidence.
- Verify tenant isolation, outlet permissions, transaction integrity and offline recovery.
- Obtain qualified jurisdiction-specific review for excise, licensing, tax, privacy and consumer engagement.
- Keep AI disabled by default; enable only after explicit governance and security approval.
