# Reference Research — Public Evidence Summary

Research date: 8 November 2026. Public vendor statements are not independent verification.

## BottleShop
- Liquor POS, packaged billing, offline-first operation, Android device support.
- Inventory intelligence, stock audits, stock transfers, multi-store dashboard.
- Cash/UPI/card reconciliation, daily reports, Tally workflow and state compliance claims.
- Advertised starting price ₹25,000/year; 7-day trial advertised.
- Source: https://bottleshop.in/liquor-pos

## PEG POS by INZOO
- 30ml/60ml/90ml/full-bottle peg billing.
- Automatic ml deduction, brand-wise inventory, low-stock alerts, stock movement/audit trail.
- Revenue dashboard, hourly/top-brand analytics, cash/UPI/card reconciliation, GST exports.
- Published ₹6,000 per user, per outlet, per year.
- Karnataka page advertises KSBCL retail and CL-9 bar licence workflows and offline mode; verify with vendor and qualified local professional.
- Sources: https://peg.inzoo.in/ ; https://peg.inzoo.in/bar-pos/karnataka

## LiKAR retail
- Barcode POS, product inventory, purchase management, supplier ledger, accounts, excise reports, state tax engine, role-based access and offline-capable POS.
- Published ₹14,999/year per shop.
- Public live demo route asks first-time users for name and phone.
- Sources: https://www.likar.app/ ; https://www.likar.app/pricing ; https://www.likar.app/demo

## LiKAR Bar
- Peg/nip/bottle selling, fractional stock deduction, scheduled pricing, state-specific registers, bar order tickets and cocktail recipes/BOM.
- Seven-day free trial advertised; price requires vendor contact.
- Karnataka-specific excise mapping not confirmed by the public page reviewed.
- Source: https://bar.likar.app/bar-management-software

## BI WORLDWIDE Channel Smart
- Alcobev case study describes onboarding, role-specific partner programmes, regional/outlet targeting, reporting, reward catalogues and auditable reward fulfilment.
- Source: https://www.biworldwide.com/en-in/our-work/case-study/enhancing-channel-loyalty-and-operational-efficiency-for-a-global-alcobev-leader/

## Bud4Trade
- App listing describes target tracking, points, schemes, self-audits, learning content, rewards and E-Tap draught ordering/service.
- Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bigcity.budtrade

## BeatRoute
- AlcoBev SFA/DMS covers on-premise, off-premise and spot selling; configurable distribution hierarchy, KPIs, visit plans, licence checks, display/pricing audits, incentives and ERP/DMS integrations.
- Source: https://beatroute.io/alcobev/

## Compliance note
Karnataka licence-to-feature mapping, reporting formats, permitted consumer/brand promotions, rewards and data sharing must be reviewed by qualified local counsel/excise professionals. Vendor marketing claims do not certify compliance.
