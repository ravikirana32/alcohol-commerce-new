# P198 — Commercial launch

## Objective
Prepare subscriptions, onboarding, support and commercial operations for a controlled launch.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Define subscription plans, entitlements and billing controls
- [ ] Complete customer onboarding, training and support escalation
- [ ] Publish service terms, privacy notices and support commitments
- [ ] Track activation, retention, reliability and customer feedback

## Acceptance evidence required
- Executed unit, integration and relevant end-to-end tests.
- Demonstrated working UI/API workflow using a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested on a clean database and supported upgrade path.
- Tenant isolation, permissions, audit and failure paths reviewed.
- Operational owner sign-off and recorded unresolved risks.

## Safety and release constraints
- Commercial launch is gated by legal, privacy, security and operational approvals

## Status
**Phase scope defined. Implementation completion and acceptance are not claimed by this phase document.**
