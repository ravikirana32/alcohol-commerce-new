# P153 — Business workflow certification

## Objective
Certify end-to-end operational journeys for licensed retail, bar and hybrid outlet profiles.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Map each role to permitted business workflows
- [ ] Test purchase-to-stock-to-sale-to-settlement journeys
- [ ] Test bottle opening, measure sale, wastage and reconciliation
- [ ] Capture operator sign-off and exception handling

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Certification is product workflow evidence, not legal or excise certification

## Delivery status
**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
