# P182 — Bar POS finalisation

## Objective
Complete bar service with configurable measures and open-bottle inventory control.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Support 30ml, 60ml, 90ml and authorised custom measures
- [ ] Deduct exact millilitres from the correct open bottle
- [ ] Support full-bottle service, table/ticket workflows and split settlement
- [ ] Record comps, wastage, breakage and manager overrides with audit history

## Acceptance evidence required
- Executed unit, integration and relevant end-to-end tests.
- Demonstrated working UI/API workflow using a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested on a clean database and supported upgrade path.
- Tenant isolation, permissions, audit and failure paths reviewed.
- Operational owner sign-off and recorded unresolved risks.

## Safety and release constraints
- Only measures and service modes permitted by the outlet licence may be enabled
