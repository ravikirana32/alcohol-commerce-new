# P164 — Partner settlement automation

## Objective
Automate partner payable, incentive, rebate and claim calculations with auditable settlement lifecycle.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Define settlement periods and source events
- [ ] Calculate eligible incentives and deductions deterministically
- [ ] Support review, dispute, approval and payout status
- [ ] Reconcile settlement statements to ledger exports

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Do not initiate real payouts until payment controls and approvals are verified

## Delivery status
**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
