# P159 — Multi-outlet rollout management

## Objective
Operationalise phased rollout across outlet groups with central governance and local accountability.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Create rollout waves, outlet readiness scorecards and owner assignments
- [ ] Validate outlet-level access and central visibility
- [ ] Test inter-outlet transfer approval and receipt
- [ ] Define rollback, incident escalation and training completion gates

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Do not expose one outlet's customer or financial data to another without permission

## Delivery status
**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
