# P157 — Karnataka bar deployment

## Objective
Prepare a controlled deployment for licensed Karnataka bars with measure-based stock control and shift reconciliation.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Verify licence permissions and service measures
- [ ] Validate open-bottle, ml deduction, comp, wastage and breakage flows
- [ ] Train bartenders and managers on shift handover
- [ ] Reconcile physical bottle/ml stock against system records

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Do not enable any sale or service mode not explicitly permitted by the outlet licence

**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
