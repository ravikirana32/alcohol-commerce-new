# P165 — Enterprise customer experience

## Objective
Improve onboarding, support, service health, training and account-level customer success workflows.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Track onboarding milestones and outlet activation
- [ ] Provide support cases, ownership and SLA clocks
- [ ] Publish knowledge and training resources
- [ ] Report account health using explainable measures

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Restrict support access to the minimum necessary customer data

**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
