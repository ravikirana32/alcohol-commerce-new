# P173 — Global enterprise customer operations

## Objective
Support global enterprise accounts with region-aware administration, service support and operational reporting.

## Deliverables
- [ ] Define global, regional and local administration boundaries
- [ ] Enforce data residency and access policies per jurisdiction
- [ ] Provide region-aware support and incident reporting
- [ ] Test cross-region recovery and tenant isolation

## Acceptance evidence
- Executed automated tests and attached report.
- Relevant API/UI workflow demonstrated in a non-production environment.
- Database migration tested both on a clean database and as an upgrade.
- Security, tenant-scope and permission checks reviewed.
- Operational owner signs off; unresolved issues are documented.

## Risks and controls
- Data residency and cross-border transfer require jurisdiction-specific review

## Delivery status
**Scope defined; implementation and acceptance are not claimed by this phase contract.**
