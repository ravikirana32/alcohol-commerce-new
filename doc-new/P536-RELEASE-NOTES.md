# P536 Release Notes — 0.536.0-dev

P536 adds an accounting source-posting foundation on top of P535: organisation-scoped account mappings, idempotent source posting, sales/refund tax legs, settlement clearing and fee legs, COGS/inventory legs, accounting period controls, posted-journal immutability and a Finance & Accounting workspace.

This is a developer-preview release. Operational event adapters, inventory cost valuation, reversal workflow, full finance reports, PostgreSQL/API/browser acceptance and accountant approval remain outstanding. No statutory tax rates or production payment behaviour are enabled.
