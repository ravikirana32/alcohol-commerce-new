# P500.9 — Inventory balances, receipts and adjustments workspace

Version: `0.500.9-dev`  
Status: developer preview; not production approved.

## Implemented
- Added Inventory Control navigation and outlet-scoped inventory workspace.
- Loads active catalogue products and stock balances for the selected outlet.
- Supports stock receipt posting with quantity, supplier/reference and reason notes.
- Supports positive/negative stock adjustments with a required reason.
- Uses existing transaction-scoped APIs that write stock movements and prevent negative balances.
- Refreshes stock balances after each successful movement.

## Limitations
- Supplier purchase orders, three-way invoice matching, approval workflows and cycle counts are not implemented in this iteration.
- No live PostgreSQL/API/browser E2E, hardware barcode scanner or stocktake pilot.
- Stock receipt endpoint is not yet tied to supplier invoice cost or accounts payable.
